You attached a report, asked three questions, and Claude says you have hit your limit until later
today. It felt like three messages. To Claude it was the whole report, inside a conversation that
grew with every question.

## What counts against your Claude usage limit

Anthropic's [advice on usage limits](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9797557-usage-limit-best-practices)
lists what spends the allowance: message length, file attachment size, current conversation length,
tools such as Research and web search, the model, the effort level, artifacts, and multi-step tasks.
The [Pro plan page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan) puts the
first three in one sentence: the number of messages you can send "will vary based on message length,
including the length of files you attach, the length of your current conversation, and the model or
feature you use."

So the limit depends on how much Claude has to read and do, not on how many times you press send. A
large attachment makes the conversation long from its first message, and everything you ask after
it is asked inside that conversation.

On paid plans there are two clocks. A session limit resets five hours after your first message, and
a weekly limit resets at a fixed time each week. Usage in claude.ai, Claude Code and Claude Desktop
all counts against the same limit, according to
[Anthropic's limits article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11647753-understanding-usage-and-length-limits).

## Why a PDF costs more than its own text

Through Anthropic's API, Claude reads a PDF twice over: the text of each page, and an image of
each page. The image is charged whether the page holds a chart or three lines of text. We took 195
PDFs from the public web and counted the 165 with a text layer with Anthropic's token counter, once
as a PDF and once as the text FileConcat extracts. The median file cost 2.81 times as much as a PDF
as it did as text. The full method is in
[How many tokens is a PDF in Claude](/blog/how-many-tokens-is-a-pdf-in-claude).

One file you can check yourself: the paper "Attention Is All You Need", 15 pages from arXiv.

These counts come from Anthropic's API. Anthropic does not publish how the claude.ai apps weigh a
PDF against your usage limit. Its [upload help page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8241126-upload-files-to-claude)
says the apps read a PDF of 101 to 1,000 pages as text only. Read the counts here as the best
available guide, not as the apps' own number.

## How to make your Claude usage limit last longer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Attach the text, not the PDF">
    Drop the PDF into FileConcat, copy the extracted text, and paste it or attach it as a `.txt`
    file. The text loses the pictures. If your question is about a chart or a scanned page, keep
    the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put files you keep asking about in a Project">
    Anthropic says "Content in projects is cached and counts less against your limits when reused."
    The cache does not last forever: "Caches expire after a period of inactivity", so the first
    message after a long break counts the files in full again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new chat when the topic changes">
    Anthropic counts the length of the current conversation against your limit, so a long chat makes
    new messages cost more. Its own advice for a long chat near the limit is to try starting a new
    conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the model and effort to the job">
    A lower effort level or a lighter model for routine questions leaves more of the limit for the
    hard ones. Turn off tools and web search a question does not need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Settings > Usage before a big job">
    Paid plans show how much of the five-hour session and the weekly limit is used, and when each
    resets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a code folder the waste is different: lock files, vendored code, build output and test files.
FileConcat's defaults leave out the ones they recognise by name, which removed a median of more
than a fifth of the tokens from fresh clones of
[60 public repositories](/blog/how-many-tokens-is-a-codebase).

The numbers will change, and so will the prices behind them. Much of your allowance goes on what
Claude reads, so send the text your question needs and nothing else.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does file size count toward the Claude usage limit?

Yes. Anthropic lists file attachment size among the factors that affect your usage limits, beside
message length and conversation length. A bigger attachment spends more of the limit.

### Why did I hit the Claude usage limit after one prompt?

One prompt can carry a lot: a long PDF, a large pasted file, a research task or web searches, or a
high effort level. On Anthropic's API a PDF is charged for an image of every page on top of its
text, and the apps read the visuals of any PDF up to 100 pages.

### When does the Claude usage limit reset?

The session limit resets in full five hours after the first message of the session. The weekly limit
resets at a fixed day and time assigned to your account. Settings > Usage shows both.

### Does starting a new chat reset the Claude usage limit?

No. The limit runs on the five-hour and weekly clocks. A new chat only makes the next messages
cheaper, because the length of the current conversation is one of the things Anthropic counts.

### Is pasted text cheaper than an uploaded PDF in Claude?

On the API almost always: 163 of 165 PDFs with a text layer counted fewer tokens as text. How the
claude.ai apps count a PDF against the limit is not published, so treat the API figure as the best
available guide, not as the apps' own number.
