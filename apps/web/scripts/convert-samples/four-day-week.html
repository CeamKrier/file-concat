What a four-day week did to our support queue

By Dana Ruiz, 2 October 2026

In March we moved the support team to a four-day week. Nobody's hours went down, the same forty hours now fit into four days, and the queue stayed open five days with a rota for Fridays.

Three months on, the first reply time went from 4 hours 10 minutes to 3 hours 40 minutes. We expected it to get worse, because Friday cover is thinner, and it did not.

What got better, and what did not

Tickets reopened less often too. Our guess is that longer days leave room to finish a hard ticket in one sitting instead of handing it over at five o'clock.

What did get worse was the Monday backlog. Friday's rota answers the urgent tickets and leaves the rest, so Monday starts with about a third more open tickets than it used to.

  • First reply: 4h 10m to 3h 40m
  • Monday backlog: up by about a third
